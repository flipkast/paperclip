# Competitive Intelligence Program

## Goal

Build and maintain a continuous competitive intelligence operation that gives
the agency a real-time view of competitor activities and market dynamics.

## Workstreams

### 1. Competitor Tracking Setup
- Identify and prioritize top 5–10 direct competitors
- Set up monitoring for competitor websites, social profiles, and ad accounts
- Establish baseline metrics for each competitor (traffic, social following, ad activity)

### 2. Weekly Intelligence Reports (Competitor Analyst)
- Competitor content activity summary (new blog posts, videos, campaigns)
- Social media activity and engagement comparison
- Ad campaign observations (new creatives, estimated spend, positioning)
- SEO ranking changes for shared keyword targets
- Product/pricing changes detected

### 3. Monthly Deep-Dive Analysis (Competitor Analyst + CMO)
- Full competitive landscape assessment
- Market share and share-of-voice trends
- Content gap analysis: topics competitors cover that we don't
- Ad strategy comparison: channel mix, messaging themes, offer structures
- Strategic recommendations based on competitive dynamics

### 4. Battlecard Maintenance (Competitor Analyst)
- Create and maintain competitive battlecards per competitor
- Track competitor strengths, weaknesses, positioning, and messaging
- Update based on new intelligence

### 5. Opportunity Identification (CMO + Competitor Analyst)
- Identify underserved market segments
- Spot competitor weaknesses to exploit
- Find content and keyword gaps for SEO team
- Identify emerging competitors early

## Success Metrics

- Intelligence report delivery: weekly, on schedule
- Competitive blind spots: zero major competitor moves missed
- Actionable insights: 3+ strategic recommendations per monthly report
- Response time: strategic alerts within 24 hours of competitor moves
