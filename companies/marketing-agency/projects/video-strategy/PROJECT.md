# Video Content Strategy & Analysis

## Goal

Develop a data-driven video content strategy powered by continuous analysis
of video performance, audience behavior, and competitive video landscape.

## Workstreams

### 1. Platform Monitoring Setup (Video Analyst)
- Configure tracking for brand's own video channels (YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, LinkedIn)
- Set up competitor video channel monitoring (top 5–10 competitor channels)
- Establish baseline metrics per channel (subscribers, avg views, engagement rate)

### 2. Performance Analysis (Video Analyst)
- Track per-video metrics: views, watch time, retention, engagement, CTR, shares
- Analyze retention curves to identify optimal video length and pacing
- Identify top-performing content themes, formats, and hooks
- Monitor audience demographics and viewing behavior changes
- Track video SEO signals (tags, descriptions, thumbnail CTR)

### 3. Trend Detection (Video Analyst)
- Monitor trending formats across TikTok, YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels
- Identify emerging content themes in the target niche
- Track viral mechanics: what hooks, formats, and topics drive sharing
- Spot platform algorithm changes and their impact on reach

### 4. Video Content Briefs (Video Analyst + Content Creator)
- Generate data-backed video content briefs weekly
- Include: topic, target platform, recommended format/length, hook suggestions,
  trending elements to incorporate, keyword targets, thumbnail strategy
- Track brief-to-performance correlation to improve future briefs

### 5. Competitive Video Analysis (Video Analyst + Competitor Analyst)
- Analyze competitor video strategy: posting cadence, formats, production quality
- Track competitor video performance trends
- Identify competitor video content gaps and opportunities
- Monitor competitor YouTube/TikTok ad creatives

## Success Metrics

- Video content performance: 15% improvement in avg engagement rate quarter-over-quarter
- Trend response time: content brief within 48 hours of identified trend
- Reporting cadence: weekly video analytics report delivered on schedule
- Brief effectiveness: 60%+ of videos following briefs outperform channel average
