# Paid Media Campaign Operations

## Goal

Run and optimize paid advertising campaigns across all major platforms
with a target ROAS and systematic A/B testing framework.

## Workstreams

### 1. Campaign Strategy (Ad Campaign Manager + CMO)
- Define target ROAS, CPA caps, and budget allocation per channel
- Map campaigns to funnel stages: awareness, consideration, conversion, retention
- Establish audience targeting strategy per platform

### 2. Campaign Execution (Ad Campaign Manager + Content Creator)
- Build campaign structures per platform (Google, Meta, LinkedIn, TikTok)
- Create ad creative briefs for Content Creator
- Set up conversion tracking and attribution
- Launch campaigns with structured A/B tests

### 3. Optimization Cycles (Ad Campaign Manager)
- Daily: monitor spend pacing, pause underperformers
- Weekly: review A/B test results, implement winners, launch new variants
- Monthly: full performance review, budget reallocation, strategy adjustments
- Quarterly: channel mix evaluation, new platform assessment

### 4. Reporting (Ad Campaign Manager + CMO)
- Weekly paid media dashboard updates
- Monthly ROAS and CPA reports per channel and campaign
- Attribution analysis: cross-channel conversion paths
- Budget utilization and efficiency reports

## Success Metrics

- ROAS: meet or exceed target per channel
- CPA: at or below target acquisition cost
- Budget pacing: within 5% of planned daily/weekly spend
- Test velocity: 3+ A/B tests running at all times
