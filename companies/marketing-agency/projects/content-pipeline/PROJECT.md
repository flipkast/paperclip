# Content Production Pipeline

## Goal

Establish a repeatable, autonomous content production pipeline that generates
SEO-optimized, brand-consistent content across all channels on a weekly cadence.

## Workstreams

### 1. Content Calendar Planning (CMO + SEO Specialist)
- Define monthly content themes aligned to marketing strategy
- Map content to funnel stages (awareness, consideration, decision)
- Assign keyword targets per content piece

### 2. Content Creation (Content Creator)
- Blog posts: 3–5 per week, 1200–2000 words, SEO-optimized
- Social media posts: 5–10 per day across platforms
- Email campaigns: 2–3 per week (nurture + promotional)
- Video scripts: 2–3 per week for short-form and long-form
- Ad copy: on-demand per Ad Campaign Manager requests

### 3. SEO Review (SEO Specialist)
- Review all blog content for keyword optimization before publish
- Optimize meta titles, descriptions, and header structure
- Ensure internal linking strategy is followed
- Monitor content performance post-publish

### 4. Distribution (Social Media Manager)
- Adapt content for each platform's format and audience
- Schedule posts at optimal engagement windows
- Cross-promote blog content through social channels
- Engage with comments and shares

### 5. Performance Tracking (Video Analyst + CMO)
- Track content performance metrics weekly
- Identify top-performing content for repurposing
- Feed performance data back into content planning

## Success Metrics

- Organic traffic growth: 20% month-over-month
- Social engagement rate: >3% average across platforms
- Email open rate: >25%, click rate: >4%
- Content production cadence: consistent weekly output
