# Competitor Research Skill

## Purpose

Provides marketing agents with structured methods for conducting competitive
intelligence research across digital channels.

## Capabilities

### Website Monitoring
- Track competitor website changes (messaging, pricing, features, design)
- Monitor competitor blog content and publishing cadence
- Detect new landing pages, product pages, and campaign pages
- Archive competitor pages for historical comparison

### Social Media Monitoring
- Track competitor posting frequency and content themes
- Monitor competitor engagement metrics and audience growth
- Identify competitor influencer partnerships
- Analyze competitor hashtag strategies

### Ad Intelligence
- Monitor competitor ad creatives via Meta Ad Library and Google Ads Transparency
- Estimate competitor ad spend patterns
- Track competitor ad messaging and positioning changes
- Identify new competitor ad campaigns and targeting signals

### SEO Competitive Analysis
- Compare keyword rankings across competitors
- Analyze competitor backlink profiles
- Identify content gaps (keywords competitors rank for that we don't)
- Monitor competitor domain authority trends

### Market Intelligence
- Track industry news and analyst reports
- Monitor competitor press releases and announcements
- Identify emerging competitors entering the space
- Gather customer review and sentiment data on competitors

## Usage

Agents using this skill should:

1. Define the competitor set to monitor
2. Specify the channels and metrics to track
3. Set alert thresholds for significant changes
4. Establish reporting cadence (daily alerts, weekly summaries, monthly deep-dives)
